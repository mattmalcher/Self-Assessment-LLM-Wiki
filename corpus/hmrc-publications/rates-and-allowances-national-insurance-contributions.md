# Rates and allowances: National Insurance contributions

This publication includes information about the rates payable for the different National Insurance classes for the current and previous 3 tax years.


## Rates and allowances: National Insurance contributions

*Source: <https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/rates-and-allowances-national-insurance-contributions/rates-and-allowances-national-insurance-contributions>*


There are different [‘classes’ of National Insurance (NI)](https://www.gov.uk/national-insurance/national-insurance-classes). The type people pay depends on their employment status and how much they earn.


New National Insurance bands and allowances are usually announced in the Chancellor of the Exchequer’s Budget or Autumn Statement.

## 4\. Class 3 National Insurance (voluntary)


You can pay [voluntary National Insurance](https://www.gov.uk/voluntary-national-insurance-contributions) to fill or avoid gaps in your record.


If you’re paying voluntary contributions for the previous 2 tax years (2023 to 2024 or 2022 to 2023\), you’ll pay the original rates for those years.


For all other years you’ll pay the current rate (2024 to 2025\).
