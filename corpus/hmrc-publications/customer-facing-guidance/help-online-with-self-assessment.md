# Help online with Self Assessment

Getting help from HMRC online is often quicker and easier than calling us.


## Use online tools


| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [Check if you need to send a Self Assessment tax return](https://www.gov.uk/check-if-you-need-tax-return) | Use this tool to help you decide if you need to complete a tax return and find out what to do next |
| [Check how to register for Self Assessment](https://www.gov.uk/register-for-self-assessment) | Use this tool to find out how to register for Self Assessment if you’re self\-employed, in a partnership or need to complete a return for another reason |
| [Check when you can expect to get a reply](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/check-when-you-can-expect-a-reply-from-hmrc) | If you’ve already contacted HMRC, find out when you can expect to get a reply |


## If you have previously registered for Self Assessment


If you did not send a tax return last year you should [check how to register](https://www.gov.uk/register-for-self-assessment), as you may need to reactivate your Self Assessment account.


Checking how to register will make sure you submit your return in the right way and mean:


- your return is processed more quickly
- any refunds or correspondence from HMRC are not delayed


## Help filling in your tax return


There is help text throughout the online tax return that gives information on completing each section. If you complete a paper tax return, you will receive guidance notes that also provide help and information.


You can also find guidance to help you understand and [fill in sections of your Self Assessment tax return](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/get-help-filling-in-your-self-assessment-tax-return). Guidance includes: 


- YouTube videos and playlists
- Self Assessment helpsheets
- recorded webinars


## Watch YouTube videos and webinars


Use [HMRC email updates, videos and webinars for Self Assessment](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/help-and-support-for-self-assessment) to:


- find YouTube playlists to help you get started with Self Assessment, fill in your tax return and understand your tax bill
- watch videos on different areas of Self Assessment
- register for and join live webinars, ask a question in the chat and get answers from one of our experts


## Tell HMRC you do not need to send a tax return


You must [tell HMRC if you believe you no longer need to send a tax return](https://www.gov.uk/self-assessment-tax-returns/no-longer-need-to-send-a-tax-return).


This might be because you’ve stopped being self\-employed or you no longer need to be registered for Self Assessment.


After you have applied to stop Self Assessment, your [personal tax account](https://www.gov.uk/personal-tax-account) will show the date and status of your application.


## Ask for help online


You can use [HMRC’s digital assistant](https://www.tax.service.gov.uk/ask-hmrc/chat/self-assessment) to ask questions about Self Assessment.


You can also ask generic questions about Self Assessment using [HMRC Customer Support on X (Twitter)](https://www.x.com/hmrccustomers) — do not include personal or financial information in your post.


## Use your HMRC online account


You can sign in to your [personal tax account](https://www.gov.uk/personal-tax-account) or [business tax account](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/sign-in-to-your-hmrc-business-tax-account) to:


- complete or amend your tax return
- check how much Self Assessment tax you owe
- make a payment or claim a refund
- find your Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR) for Self Assessment


You can also [download the HMRC app](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/download-the-hmrc-app) onto a mobile device (for example, your phone or tablet).


## Get extra support


If a health condition or personal circumstances makes it difficult to use our online services, you can find out how to [get help from HMRC if you need extra support](https://www.gov.uk/get-help-hmrc-extra-support).


HMRC provides funding to voluntary and community sector organisations so they can help you if you need extra support with your tax. You can [contact a voluntary or community sector organisation](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/voluntary-and-community-sector-organisations-who-can-give-you-extra-support) for free Self Assessment help and advice.
