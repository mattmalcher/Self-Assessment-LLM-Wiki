# Check when you can expect a reply from HMRC

The information in this tool is updated weekly.


You can use this tool for queries or requests you’ve made about:


- Child Benefit
- Construction Industry Scheme (CIS)
- Corporation Tax
- employers’ PAYE
- Income Tax
- Inheritance Tax
- National Insurance
- public services pensions remedy
- Self Assessment (including Unique Taxpayer References (UTRs))
- tax credits
- VAT


If you’re an agent, you can also check how long it will take HMRC to:


- process an application for authority to act on behalf of a client
- amend your agent details


 [Start now](http://www.tax.service.gov.uk/guidance/Check-when-you-can-expect-a-reply-from-HMRC) 


If your query is about anything else, [contact HMRC](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/hm-revenue-customs/contact).
