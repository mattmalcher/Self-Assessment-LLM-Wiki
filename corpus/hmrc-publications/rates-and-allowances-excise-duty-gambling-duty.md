# Rates and allowances: Excise Duty — Gambling Duty

The attached document is classified by HMRC as guidance and contains information about the rates payable for Gaming Duty, General Betting Duty, Lottery Duty, Machine Games Duty, Pool Betting Duty and Remote Gaming Duty.


## Excise Duty — Gambling Duty rates

*Source: <https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/rates-and-allowance-excise-duty-gambling-duty/excise-duty-gambling-duty-rates>*


## Gaming Duty


| Gross [gaming yield](https://www.gov.uk/gaming-duty) |  | Accounting periods beginning on or after 1 April 2026 Tax rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The first £2,686,000 |  | 15% |
| The next £1,852,000 |  | 20% |
| The next £3,243,000 |  | 30% |
| The next £6,845,000 |  | 40% |
| Remainder |  | 50% |


## General Betting Duty


|  | Tax year 2026 to 2027 |
| --- | --- |
| Percentage of ‘net stake receipts’ (essentially the gross profits from bookmaking)for fixed odds bets and totalisator on horse or dog races | 15% |
| Percentage of ‘net stake receipts’ for financial spread bets | 3% |
| Percentage of ‘net stake receipts’ for all other spread bets | 10% |


Read about [General Betting Duty](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/general-betting-duty-pool-betting-duty-and-remote-gaming-duty).


## Lottery Duty

## Machine Games Duty


| Cost of play and cash prize levels | Tax year 2026 to 2027 |
| --- | --- |
| Percentage of the net takings from dutiable machine games with a maximum cost of playnot more than 20p and a maximum cash prize not more than £10 | 5% |
| Percentage of net takings from machines which are not Type 1 machines but where thecost to play cannot exceed £5 | 20% |
| Percentage of net takings from dutiable machine games where the maximum cost to playcan exceed £5 | 25% |


Read about [Machine Games Duty](https://www.gov.uk/machine-games-duty).
