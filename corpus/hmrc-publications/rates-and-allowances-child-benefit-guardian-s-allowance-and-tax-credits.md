# Rates and allowances: Child Benefit, Guardian's Allowance and tax credits

This document is classified by HMRC as guidance and contains information about the rates payable for:


- Child Benefit
- Guardian’s Allowance


## Tax credits ended on 5 April 2025


You can also get information about previous tax credits income thresholds and the childcare element for:


- Working Tax Credit — rates up to 5 April 2025
- Child Tax Credit — rates up to 5 April 2025


Read more information about [tax credits which ended on 5 April 2025](https://www.gov.uk/tax-credits-have-ended).
