# Rates and allowances: Aggregates Levy

The attachment shows the rate of Aggregates Levy due per tonne when aggregate (sand, gravel or rock) is commercially exploited.


## Rates and allowances — Aggregates Levy

*Source: <https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/rates-and-allowances-aggregates-levy/rates-and-allowances-aggregates-levy>*


You must [register for the levy](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/aggregates-levy-register-or-change-your-details) if for commercial purposes you:


- exploit taxable aggregate in England, Wales or Northern Ireland
- move taxable aggregate to England, Wales or Northern Ireland from Scotland


| Tax year | Taxable aggregate |
| --- | --- |
| Rate from 1 April 2024 | £2\.03 per tonne |
| Rate from 1 April 2025 | £2\.08 per tonne |
| Rate from 1 April 2026 | £2\.16 per tonne |
